The rules

Short version: bids are binding, everything opens at $1, money moves through escrow, and listing costs $1, $5 or $10 — we take no commission on the sale.

1 · Bidding

Are bids binding?Yes — a winning bid is a contract

Proxy / auto-biddingSet a ceiling; we bid the minimum needed

Auto-bidding works the way a live auction house does. You name the highest number you would pay; the system only ever bids one increment above the current price, and it stops the moment someone goes past your ceiling. Nobody sees your maximum — not the seller, not other bidders.

2 · The anti-snipe clock

Any bid placed in the final two minutes pushes the closing time out by two minutes. That repeats for as long as people keep bidding. Sniping a lot in the last second wins you nothing here — the price ends where demand actually is, not where reflexes are fastest.

A lot can extend indefinitely. The longest auction on record here ran 41 minutes past its scheduled close and finished 3.2× above the price at the original deadline.

Nobody transfers a domain on a promise. The buyer's money sits with the escrow agent while the domain, repository, hosting, analytics, payment processor and mailing list move across. If the seller cannot deliver what the listing claimed, the buyer gets a full refund and the seller covers the escrow fee.

5 · What may not be listed

  • ✗ Assets you do not own outright, or that carry an active lien or partnership claim.
  • ✗ Traffic or revenue generated by bots, click farms or paid-to-click schemes.
  • ✗ Sites built on scraped personal data, or mailing lists collected without consent.
  • ✗ Trademark-infringing domains and typosquats.
  • ✗ Anything whose revenue depends on malware, phishing or deceptive billing.
  • ✗ Adult, gambling and pharmaceutical assets — payment partners will not process them.
Misstated numbers void the sale. If a buyer proves within the inspection window that revenue or traffic was materially misrepresented, the escrow refunds in full and the seller is removed from the marketplace.
