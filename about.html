About

Most side projects die in a folder.

Not because they were bad, but because selling one was harder than abandoning it. Brokers want a $500k asset. Marketplaces want a valuation you cannot produce. So the domain lapses, the repo goes private, and something that made $400 a month for two years quietly stops existing.

What we changed

BidYourSite is an auction house for small internet businesses. Every lot opens at one dollar. There is no valuation call, no listing review queue, no minimum revenue and no exclusivity agreement. You describe the asset, pick how long it runs, and the market answers the only question that matters.

The dollar is deliberate. It is low enough that listing a dead project is a shrug rather than a decision, and it guarantees the auction starts where auctions work best — below what anyone thinks the thing is worth.

Numbers on this page come from the live marketplace and move as bidding does.
Principles

Four things we will not do

✗

Charge 15%

Our fee is 5% and stops at $500. A marketplace should not take a bigger cut of your exit than the tax office.

✗

Hide reserves

No secret floor prices. A bidder who beats the increment has genuinely won the lot at that price.

✗

Let snipers win

Late bids extend the clock. Price should reflect what someone will pay, not who has the fastest connection.

✗

Touch your money

Funds sit with a licensed escrow agent from the moment the hammer falls until the buyer signs off.

The honest caveats

  • A $1 open can end at $1. Thin listings with no traffic sometimes attract nobody. You lose a dollar and learn something.
  • Bids are binding. This trips people up. Do not bid to "see what happens".
  • Verification is optional but decisive. Self-reported lots close far lower, and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • We are not a broker. Nobody is going to talk your asset up on a call. The listing you write is the pitch.
