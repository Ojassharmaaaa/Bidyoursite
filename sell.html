Seller tips

  • Post the ugly numbers. Churn, refunds, the month revenue dipped. Buyers discount what they cannot see.
  • 7 days beats 1 day for anything over $1,000 — it takes a weekend for serious buyers to surface.
  • Skip the instant-buy price unless you genuinely want out fast; it caps your upside.
  • Answer questions within a day. Lots with an active seller thread take 3× more bids.
